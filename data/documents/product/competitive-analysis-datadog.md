## Executive Summary

### Pricing Model Differences

| Aspect | CloudSignal | Datadog |

## Competitive Wins/Losses

### Where We Win

1. **Price-sensitive enterprises**
   - "CloudSignal is 80% cheaper for the same functionality"
   - Win rate: 65% when price is primary factor

2. **Simplicity seekers**
   - "Datadog is too complex for our team size"
   - Win rate: 72% for teams < 20 engineers

3. **Alerting-focused use cases**
   - "CloudSignal's alerting is more intuitive"
   - Win rate: 70% when alerting is primary need

### Where We Lose

1. **Full observability suite**
   - "We need APM + Logs + RUM in one platform"
   - Loss rate: 80% for full-stack requirements

2. **Brand/enterprise validation**
   - "We need a proven vendor for compliance"
   - Loss rate: 60% for Fortune 500

3. **Specific integrations**
   - "Datadog has 500+ integrations, you have 100"
   - Loss rate: 55% for complex tech stacks

## Competitive Positioning

## Strategic Recommendations
