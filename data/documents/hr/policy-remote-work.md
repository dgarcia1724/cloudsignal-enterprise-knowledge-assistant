# Remote Work Policy

**Version:** 1.1.0
**Last Updated:** 2024-09-15
**Owner:** People Operations
**Status:** Active

## Overview

CloudSignal embraces a flexible, remote-first work culture. This policy outlines expectations and guidelines for remote work arrangements.

## Work Arrangements

### Remote-First Philosophy

CloudSignal is a remote-first company. All roles are designed to be performed remotely unless specifically designated as on-site.

### Arrangement Types

| Type | Description | Approval |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Fully Remote | Work from anywhere in approved locations | Default |
| Hybrid | Mix of remote and office (where available) | Manager |
| On-Site | Required office presence | Role-specific |

### Approved Work Locations

Employees may work from:
- United States (all states)
- Canada
- United Kingdom
- Germany
- Other locations with VP People Ops approval

**Note:** Work location affects tax withholding and benefits eligibility. Notify HR before relocating.

## Core Hours & Availability

### Core Collaboration Hours

All employees should be available during **core hours**:
- **US Teams:** 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM Eastern Time
- **EU Teams:** 2:00 PM - 6:00 PM GMT

### Flexibility Outside Core Hours

Outside core hours, employees may:
- Set their own schedule
- Block focus time
- Adjust for personal commitments

### Communication Expectations

| Channel | Expected Response Time |
|---------|----------------------|
| Slack (direct) | Within 4 hours during work day |
| Slack (channel) | Within 24 hours |
| Email | Within 24-48 hours |
| Urgent (PagerDuty) | Per on-call SLA |

## Home Office Requirements

### Minimum Requirements

- Reliable high-speed internet (25+ Mbps)
- Quiet, private workspace for calls
- Ergonomic setup (desk, chair)
- Secure environment for confidential work

### Equipment Provided

CloudSignal provides:
- MacBook Pro laptop
- External monitor (upon request)
- Keyboard and mouse
- Headset for video calls

### Home Office Stipend

- **Monthly stipend:** $100 for internet, utilities, supplies
- **One-time setup:** $500 for ergonomic equipment (desk, chair)

## Meetings & Collaboration

### Meeting Best Practices

1. **Camera on** for team meetings (unless bandwidth issues)
2. **Default to async** when possible
3. **Record important meetings** for those who can't attend
4. **Respect time zones** when scheduling

### In-Person Gatherings

- **Team offsites:** 2x per year, company-funded
- **Company all-hands:** 1x per year, optional attendance
- **Ad-hoc travel:** Pre-approved, expensed

## Security Requirements

### Device Security

- Full disk encryption enabled
- Screen lock after 5 minutes
- VPN required for production access
- No public WiFi without VPN

### Data Handling

- No confidential data on personal devices
- Secure document disposal (shred sensitive docs)
- Report lost/stolen devices immediately

## Performance Expectations

Remote work does not change performance expectations. Employees are evaluated on:

1. **Output quality** - Meeting deliverables and goals
2. **Communication** - Responsiveness and clarity
3. **Collaboration** - Teamwork and participation
4. **Reliability** - Consistent availability during work hours

## Travel & Co-Working

### Workation Policy

Employees may work from non-home locations (workation) with:
- Manager notification for trips > 1 week
- No impact on availability during core hours
- Compliance with data security requirements

### Co-Working Spaces

- Reimbursed up to $300/month with manager approval
- Must meet security requirements (private calls, secure network)

## Policy Updates

This policy is reviewed annually. Significant changes are communicated via email and All-Hands.
