## Introduction

CloudSignal offers a comprehensive benefits package designed to support your health, wealth, and well-being. This document summarizes our 2025 benefits.

## Health Insurance

### Medical Plans

| Plan | Monthly Premium (Employee) | Deductible | Coverage |
|------|---------------------------|------------|----------|
| PPO Premium | $0 | $500 | 90/70 |
| PPO Standard | $0 | $1,500 | 80/60 |
| HDHP + HSA | $0 | $3,000 | 80/60 |

**Coverage Details:**
- In-network/Out-of-network shown as percentages
- Family coverage available (employee cost varies)
- Pre-existing conditions covered
- No waiting period

### Dental Insurance

| Plan | Premium | Annual Max |
|------|---------|------------|
| Delta Dental PPO | $0 | $2,000 |

**Includes:**
- Preventive: 100% covered (2 cleanings/year)
- Basic: 80% covered
- Major: 50% covered
- Orthodontia: 50% (lifetime max $2,000)

### Vision Insurance

| Plan | Premium | Exam | Frames |
|------|---------|------|--------|
| VSP | $0 | $10 copay | $150 allowance |

**Includes:**
- Annual eye exam
- Frames or contacts allowance
- Lens coverage

## Retirement

### 401(k) Plan

- **Eligibility:** Immediately upon hire
- **Company match:** 100% of first 4% contributed
- **Vesting:** Immediate (no waiting period)
- **Provider:** Fidelity

**2025 Contribution Limits:**
- Employee: $23,000 ($30,500 if 50+)
- Employer match: Up to 4% of salary

### Investment Options

- Target Date Funds
- Index Funds (S&P 500, Total Market, International)
- Bond Funds
- Self-directed brokerage available
