## Compensation Philosophy

CloudSignal targets the **75th percentile** of market compensation for technology companies. We believe competitive pay attracts and retains top talent.

### Total Compensation Components

1. **Base Salary** - Fixed annual compensation
2. **Equity** - Stock options with 4-year vesting
3. **Bonus** - Performance-based (where applicable)
4. **Benefits** - Valued at ~$15,000/year

## Engineering Levels

### Level Framework

| Level | Title | Experience | Scope |
|-------|-------|------------|-------|
| L3 | Software Engineer | 0-2 years | Individual contributor, defined tasks |
| L4 | Software Engineer II | 2-4 years | IC, owns small features |
| L5 | Senior Software Engineer | 4-7 years | IC, owns large features/systems |
| L6 | Staff Software Engineer | 7-10 years | Technical leadership, cross-team |
| L7 | Principal Engineer | 10+ years | Company-wide technical direction |

## 2025 Salary Bands

| Level | Title | Min | Mid | Max |

### Equity Grants (Stock Options)

| Level | New Hire Grant | Refresh (Annual) |
|-------|---------------|------------------|
| L3 | 5,000 options | 1,000 options |
| L4 | 10,000 options | 2,500 options |
| L5 | 20,000 options | 5,000 options |
| L6 | 40,000 options | 10,000 options |
| L7 | 75,000 options | 20,000 options |

**Equity Details:**
- 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff
- Exercise price: Fair market value at grant
- Options expire 10 years from grant

## Geographic Adjustments

Base salary adjustments by location tier:

| Tier | Locations | Adjustment |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Tier 1 | SF Bay Area, NYC, Seattle | 100% (baseline) |
| Tier 2 | Boston, LA, Denver, Austin | 95% |
| Tier 3 | Other US metros | 90% |
| Tier 4 | US remote/rural | 85% |
| International | Case by case | Varies |

**Example:** L5 Senior Engineer
- Tier 1 (SF): $175K - $225K
- Tier 3 (Columbus): $157.5K - $202.5K

## Position in Band

Where an employee falls within their band depends on:

| Factor | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Experience level | 30% |
| Performance rating | 30% |
| Scope of role | 20% |
| Market conditions | 20% |

### Band Position Guidelines

- **Min-25%:** New to level, developing
- **25%-50%:** Meeting expectations
- **50%-75%:** Exceeding expectations
- **75%-Max:** Top performer, ready for next level

## Promotion Guidelines

### Compensation Adjustments at Promotion

| Promotion | Typical Increase |
|-----------|-----------------|
| L3 → L4 | 15-20% |
| L4 → L5 | 12-18% |
| L5 → L6 | 15-20% |
| L6 → L7 | 12-18% |

**Note:** Promoted employees should land at minimum in new band, typically at 25-50% position.

## Annual Compensation Review

### Review Cycle

- **Timing:** February each year
- **Effective:** March 1

### Increase Guidelines

| Performance | Merit Increase | Equity Refresh |
|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| Below Expectations | 0% | 0% |
| Meets Expectations | 3-4% | 50% of target |
| Exceeds Expectations | 5-7% | 100% of target |
| Outstanding | 8-10% | 150% of target |

## Manager Guidelines

### When Hiring

1. Determine appropriate level based on experience and interview
2. Consider current team equity and internal fairness
3. Target 50th percentile for most hires
4. Above 75th requires VP approval

### When Promoting

1. Confirm employee meets next-level criteria
2. Determine appropriate band position
3. Equity refresh at promotion target
4. Communicate total compensation change
