## 2. Key Wins & Challenges

### Major Wins

1. **Enterprise Breakthrough**
   - Closed 3 deals >$100K ACV
   - Fortune 500 logo: GlobalTech Industries ($180K)
   - Average enterprise deal: $125K (up from $85K)

2. **Product-Market Fit Strengthening**
   - NPS increased to 52 (from 45 in Q3)
   - Feature adoption at 68%
   - Support tickets down 15%

3. **Team Scaling**
   - Added 18 team members (net)
   - Key hires: VP Sales, Staff Engineers (2)
   - Attrition: 4% (below industry 12%)

4. **Operational Efficiency**
   - Burn rate reduced 7%
   - Cloud costs optimized (-$26K/month)
   - CAC payback improved to 11 months

### Challenges

1. **Sales Cycle Length**
   - Enterprise deals averaging 75 days (target: 60)
   - Security reviews adding 2-3 weeks
   - Action: Dedicated security documentation team

2. **Competitor Pressure**
   - Datadog aggressive on pricing
   - Lost 3 deals to Datadog discounting
   - Action: Value-selling training, case studies

3. **EU Expansion Delayed**
   - Data residency requirements complex
   - GDPR compliance gap identified
   - Action: Prioritized in 2025 roadmap
