## Executive Summary

Q4 2024 was CloudSignal's strongest quarter to date. We achieved $4.2M in ARR (up 18% QoQ), added 47 new customers, and reduced churn to 1.2%. The company is well-positioned for continued growth in 2025.

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Q4 2024 | Q3 2024 | Change |
|--------|---------|---------|--------|
| ARR | $4.2M | $3.56M | +18% |
| MRR | $350K | $297K | +18% |
| Net New ARR | $640K | $480K | +33% |
| Customers | 247 | 200 | +24% |
| Gross Margin | 78% | 76% | +2pp |
| Burn Rate | $420K/mo | $450K/mo | -7% |
