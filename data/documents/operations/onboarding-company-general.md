## Welcome to CloudSignal!

Congratulations on joining the CloudSignal team! We're excited to have you. This guide will help you get up to speed in your first days and weeks.

## About CloudSignal

### Our Mission

To make cloud monitoring accessible, intuitive, and actionable for every engineering team.

### Our Values

1. **Customer Obsession** - We succeed when our customers succeed
2. **Simplicity** - Simple is better than complex
3. **Ownership** - We take responsibility and follow through
4. **Transparency** - We default to open communication
5. **Continuous Improvement** - We're always learning and growing

### Company Overview

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Founded | 2019 |
| Headquarters | San Francisco, CA |
| Employees | ~300 |
| Customers | 500+ |
| Work Style | Remote-first |

### Our Product

CloudSignal is a cloud monitoring and alerting platform that helps engineering teams:

- Monitor infrastructure, applications, and services
- Set up intelligent alerts that reduce noise
- Collaborate on incidents in real-time
- Analyze metrics and trends
- Integrate with 100+ tools

## Your First Week

### Week 1 Goals

1. Complete all required trainings
2. Meet your immediate team members
3. Understand team goals and priorities
4. Set up development environment (if applicable)
5. Shadow team meetings

### Required Training

| Training | Duration | Deadline |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Security Awareness | 1 hour | Day 1 |
| Anti-Harassment | 45 min | Day 2 |
| Company Culture | 30 min | Day 3 |
| Product Overview | 1 hour | Day 5 |
| Department Onboarding | Varies | Week 1 |

### Meetings to Schedule

| Meeting | With | Duration |
|---------|------|----------|
| 1:1 with Manager | Your manager | 30 min (recurring) |
| Meet the Team | Team members | 30 min each |
| Onboarding Buddy | Assigned buddy | 30 min (recurring) |
| Skip-level | Manager's manager | 30 min |

At the end of your first month, you'll meet with your manager to discuss:

## Working at CloudSignal

### Work Hours

- **Core hours:** 10 AM - 3 PM in your timezone
- **Flexibility:** Start/end when works for you
- **Overlap:** Ensure 3+ hours with your team

### Remote Work

We're remote-first. Key expectations:

- Reliable internet connection
- Quiet space for video calls
- Available during core hours
- Camera on for team meetings

### Time Off

| Type | Allowance |
|------|-----------|
| PTO | 20-30 days (tenure-based) |
| Sick Leave | Unlimited (use responsibly) |
| Holidays | 11 company holidays |
| Parental Leave | 16 weeks paid |

To request time off:
1. Request in Lattice
2. Notify your manager
3. Update Slack status
4. Set up out-of-office

## FAQ

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Payday is the 15th and last day of each month via direct deposit.

**Q: How do I request time off?**
A: Submit requests through Lattice and notify your manager.

**Q: What if I have IT issues after hours?**
A: Post in #it-help; urgent issues go to on-call via PagerDuty.

**Q: Can I work from another country?**
A: Some countries are approved. Check with HR for your situation.

**Q: How do I order office supplies?**
A: Use the home office stipend ($100/month) or request through #facilities.
